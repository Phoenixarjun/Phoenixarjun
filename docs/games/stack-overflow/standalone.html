HOW HIGH CAN YOU BUILD?

A server block moves across the top of your tower. Release it as close to the existing layer as possible; any overhang is cut away. Narrow platforms eventually become too difficult to land on.

01 — AIM

Track the projected landing marker. The block retains a small amount of momentum when released.

02 — DROP

Press Space or click/tap the arena to release. Gravity does the rest.

03 — RECOVER

Perfect drops preserve or slightly restore width and charge your focus meter.

04 — SURVIVE

Later sectors add speed and wind. If the overlap becomes too small, the stack fails.
