DEFEND THE CORE.

Hostile packets arrive along two separate network paths. Place defense modules on glowing build nodes to intercept traffic before it reaches the core.

  1. Choose Firewall, Rate Limiter, Pulse Node, or IDS Scanner.
  2. Click an available node to deploy. Modules automatically target incoming traffic.
  3. Launch a wave. Earn bandwidth credits from destroyed packets; upgrade and reposition defenses between waves.
  4. Survive 30 authored waves across six threat acts, featuring splitter packets, jammers, regenerators, and six major boss assaults. Build Scatter Cannons for triple-shot volleys or Rail Lances for piercing blasts. Three command protocols—EMP, Overclock, and Core Shield—consume credits and have cooldowns. On a phone, swipe the map horizontally to reach distant nodes.
